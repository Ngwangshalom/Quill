# Code of Conduct

Quill aims to be a language a beginner can read and a team can trust. The community around it should feel the same: welcoming to beginners and trustworthy for everyone.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in Quill a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to programming or to open source.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it constructively.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every Quill space: the repository, issues, pull requests, Discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers at **quillspace@gmail.com**. Every report will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, code or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
